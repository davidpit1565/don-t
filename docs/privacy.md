# DON'T PRESS: Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 20 October 2026
**Developer:** David Pitchkhadze · davidpit2008@gmail.com

DON'T PRESS is built to work entirely on your device. **It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.**

## What the app stores
Two numbers, saved only on your device, in the app's local storage (`UserDefaults`):

- your **personal best** (the highest number of presses you reached in one session), and
- your **total presses** (a running count across sessions).

Your *current run* is kept in memory only and starts again at zero every time the app is launched fresh.
These numbers are not linked to your identity. The app has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.

## What the app does not do
- No network requests: the app does not connect to any server.
- No analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs. No third-party code at all.
- No permissions are requested.

## Sharing
When you tap **Share personal best**, the app creates an image on your device and opens the standard iOS share sheet. The image goes only where *you* choose to send it. The developer never receives it.

## Retention and deletion
The two stored numbers stay on your device until you delete the app, which removes them. The developer has no copy.

## Children
The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this page with a new effective date.
