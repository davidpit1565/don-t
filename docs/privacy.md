# DON'T PRESS: Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** _set this to the day version 1.1 is released_ (previous version: 20 October 2026)
**Developer:** David Pitchkhadze · davidpit2008@gmail.com

DON'T PRESS is built to work entirely on your device. **It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.**
The developer runs no server and never receives anything from the app.

This policy covers version 1.1 and later. Items marked *(1.1)* are new in version 1.1; everything else also applies to version 1.0.

## What the app stores, and where
The app saves a small amount of game state **only on your device**, in the app's local storage (`UserDefaults`):

- your **personal best** (the highest number of presses you reached in one session);
- your **total presses** (a running count across sessions);
- the **button style** you chose;
- a **cached copy of your Pro status** (whether this device has already confirmed with Apple that you own the optional Pro purchase), so Pro works offline;
- whether **sound** is on or off *(1.1)*;
- which **toy** you selected (for example the button, the egg or the balloon) *(1.1)*;
- which **creatures and reactions you have discovered** (your Reactiondex collection) *(1.1)*;
- your **daily challenge and day-streak data**: which days you completed the daily challenge, your current streak and your best streak *(1.1)*.

Your *current run* is kept in memory only and starts again at zero every time the app is launched fresh.

None of this is linked to your identity. The app has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, microphone, or any other personal information.

## How the new 1.1 features work *(1.1)*
- **Sounds** are generated on your device while the app runs. The app does not record audio and does not use the microphone.
- **The daily challenge** is produced on your device from the current date (your device's clock). Your results and streak are not sent anywhere. There is no server, leaderboard or ranking.
- **Toys and the Reactiondex** are part of the app. Progress is saved only on your device.

## What the app does not do
- No network requests of its own: the app does not connect to any server run by the developer. (Only Apple's App Store handles the optional purchase.)
- No analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs. No third-party code at all.
- No accounts, no sign-in, no cloud sync run by the developer.
- No permissions are requested.

## Purchases
The app offers an optional one-time purchase ("Pro"). Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment details, Apple ID, or name, and the app does not send any purchase information anywhere else. The app only asks Apple whether this Apple ID owns Pro, and keeps the answer on the device (see above). Restoring a purchase also goes through Apple.

## Sharing
When you tap a share button, the app creates an image on your device and opens the standard iOS share sheet. The image goes only where *you* choose to send it. The developer never receives it.

## Retention and deletion
Everything listed above stays on your device until you delete the app, which removes it from the device. The developer has no copy and no access. If you use iPhone or iCloud device backups, the app's local data may be included in your own backup, which is controlled by you and Apple, not by the developer.

## Children
The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
