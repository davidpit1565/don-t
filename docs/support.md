## FAQ

### The basics

**What counts as a "run"?**
Every press since the app was launched. When the app is closed fully and opened again, the run starts at zero. Sending the app to the background, coming back, or opening and cancelling the share sheet does *not* reset it.

**What is "Personal best"?**
The highest run you have ever reached on this device. It updates the moment you pass it.

**What is "Total presses"?**
Every press you have ever made on this device, across all sessions.

**Why is the Share button greyed out?**
There is nothing to share until you have pressed at least once.

**Is my score online or ranked against other people?**
No. Everything stays on your device. The app has no accounts, leaderboard, or server, and the daily challenge does not compare you with anyone.

### Daily challenge and streak *(1.1)*

**What is the daily challenge?**
One small challenge per day, the same idea as a daily word puzzle: a new one appears each day, and you can finish it once. It is created on your iPhone from today's date, so it works offline.

**How does the day streak work?**
Finish the daily challenge on consecutive days to grow your streak. Missing a day ends the current streak; your best streak is kept. "Day" means a calendar day on your iPhone's clock.

**I travelled / changed my clock and my streak looks wrong.**
The challenge and streak follow your iPhone's date and time zone. Changing the clock or crossing time zones can skip or repeat a day. Email me with what happened and I will look into it.

**Will the app remind me to keep my streak?**
No. The app sends no notifications.

### Toys and the Reactiondex *(1.1)*

**What are the toys?**
Besides the classic button there are other things not to press: an egg that hatches creatures and a balloon that pops. Switch toys from the main screen. Your counters keep working with every toy.

**What is the Reactiondex?**
A collection of every reaction and creature you have discovered. New entries appear as you find them by playing. Your collection is saved only on this iPhone.

**"The button fights back": did something break?**
Probably not. At certain milestones the button responds with a short set-piece. That is on purpose. Keep pressing (or don't).

### Sound *(1.1)*

**How do I turn sound on or off?**
Use the sound toggle in the app. Your choice is remembered. The app's sounds are generated on the device; it never uses the microphone.

**I hear no sound.**
Check that sound is on in the app, the volume is up, and the ring/silent switch (or Action button setting) is not set to silent.

### Button styles, Pro and restoring purchases

**How do I unlock button styles and achievements?**
Tap the trophy icon at the top right of the main screen. Locked items show what you need to do and your progress. Styles unlock at: Blue Mistake (run of 10), Stealth (run of 50), Radioactive (run of 250), Gold Standard (500 total presses).

**What is Pro?**
Pro is an optional one-time purchase (no subscription, no ads either way). In version 1.0 it unlocks all five button styles right away; you can also unlock them for free by playing. The app shows exactly what Pro includes, and the price, before you buy.

**How do I restore Pro on a new iPhone or after reinstalling?**
Open the trophy screen, tap a locked style or the Pro row, and choose "Restore Purchases". Use the same Apple ID you bought it with. Restoring is free.

**Refunds or payment problems?**
Apple handles payments and refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com. For anything else, email me.

### Data

**Do I lose my progress if I delete the app?**
Deleting the app removes your numbers, chosen style, streak and collection from your device. The app has no account, sync or cloud service of its own. If you use iPhone or iCloud device backups, this data can be restored along with the rest of the app's data when you restore a backup. Pro can always be restored with "Restore Purchases".

**The button does not vibrate.**
Haptics need an iPhone with a Taptic Engine, and "System Haptics" must be on in Settings → Sounds & Haptics. The app works the same without them.
